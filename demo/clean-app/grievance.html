Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Established pursuant to Section 8(10) and Section 13 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Rule 14(3) of the DPDP Rules, and Rule 5(9) of the SPDI Rules, 2011.

Stated Response Timeline: 30 Days

Commitment: All grievances submitted through this portal or to our Grievance Officer will be formally acknowledged within 48 hours and redressed within a maximum response timeline of 30 days (one month), adhering to the stricter statutory benchmark.

Designated Grievance Officer

  • Title: Grievance Redressal Officer
  • Designated Role: Grievance Officer
  • Contact Email: grievance@example.com
  • Secondary Officer (DPO): dpo@example.com

Submit a Grievance

Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India

In accordance with Section 13(3) of the DPDP Act, if your grievance is not redressed within the stated 30-day timeline, or if you are dissatisfied with the resolution, you are entitled to approach the Data Protection Board of India to register an escalation after exhausting this internal mechanism.
