Welcome to Clean Demo App

This is a test fixture demonstrating privacy engineering baselines aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the SPDI Rules, 2011.

Privacy First Architecture

All planted anti-patterns from the flawed test fixture have been resolved in this application:

  • Static Assets: Self-hosted fonts with zero third-party tracking scripts or remote CDN calls.
  • Consent: Independent, unbundled, unchecked consent toggles per distinct processing purpose.
  • Notice & Governance: Clear notice with explicit withdrawal and grievance routes.
  • Principal Rights: Dedicated workflows for data access, correction, erasure, and nomination.
  • Child Protection: Date of birth collection with a verifiable parental consent workflow.
  • Security: Strong Argon2id hashing, HSTS enforcement, restricted cloud storage, and 365-day audit retention.
