Data Protection Notice

Issued in compliance with Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rule 3 of the DPDP Rules.

Application Communication Link: Return to the Main Web Application Dashboard or contact our data protection team directly through our communication desk.

1. Personal Data Processed and Specified Purposes

We process your username, verified email address, and authentication credentials solely to provide account authentication, secure session handling, and service delivery. Optional marketing and telemetry processing occurs only upon your explicit, affirmative opt-in consent.

2. Route for Consent Withdrawal

You have the statutory right under Section 6(4) of the DPDP Act to withdraw your consent at any time, with ease comparable to giving consent. You may withdraw consent through:

  • Your account dashboard preferences under "Privacy Settings".
  • Submitting an email request directly to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@example.com with the subject line "Consent Withdrawal Request".

3. Route for Exercising Data Principal Rights

Under Sections 11, 12, and 14 of the DPDP Act, you may exercise your rights to access summary personal data, correct inaccurate details, erase obsolete information, and nominate representatives. Exercise your rights anytime via our dedicated Data Principal Rights Portal or by emailing our designated privacy office.

4. Route for Grievance Redressal and Board Complaints

If you have any concern or grievance regarding the processing of your personal data:

  • Internal Redressal: First submit your concern through our Grievance Redressal Mechanism or contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@example.com. We are committed to responding within 30 days.
  • Board Complaint: In accordance with Section 13(3) of the DPDP Act, if your grievance is unresolved through our internal mechanism, you may file a formal complaint with the Data Protection Board of India via their designated digital portal.

5. Authorized Contact Person Details

For inquiries, notices, or data-related communication:

  • Role: Data Protection Officer & Grievance Officer
  • Contact Email: dpo@example.com
  • Alternative Escalation: grievance@example.com
  • Direct Contact Page: Contact Us
