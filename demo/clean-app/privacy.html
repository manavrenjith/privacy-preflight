Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-10-30 | Version: 1.0 (Test Fixture Baseline)

This Privacy Policy is published in accordance with Rule 4 of the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect (What is Collected)

We collect and process the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity and Contact Data: Username, email address, and date of birth provided during registration.
  • Parental Consent Information: Guardian full name and verified guardian email address for registered users under 18 years of age.
  • Technical Data: In-app access logs and audit trails strictly retained for platform security.

2. Purpose of Collection and Usage (Why We Collect)

Your personal data is collected and processed exclusively for specified, explicit, and lawful purposes:

  • To administer user accounts, authenticate identity, and provide access to core features.
  • To verify parental consent for minors in accordance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act.
  • To send service notifications, security alerts, and administrative messages.
  • With optional, unbundled consent: to send product newsletters or aggregate diagnostic telemetry.

3. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Personal data may only be disclosed under the following controlled circumstances:

  • To authorized data processors who operate under strict contractual data protection obligations in compliance with Section 8(2) of the DPDP Act.
  • To law enforcement or regulatory authorities when strictly required by a court order or applicable Indian law.
  • No cross-border transfers occur without fulfilling statutory transfer prerequisites under Section 16 of the DPDP Act.

4. Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) enforced with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) on all network communications.
  • State-of-the-art Argon2id algorithm for password hashing and authentication protection.
  • Role-based access controls and restrictive storage rules prohibiting public unauthorized write or read access.
  • Comprehensive security access logging with a 365-day retention policy aligned with Rule 6(1)(e) and Rule 8(3).

5. Grievance Redressal and Named Grievance Officer

In accordance with Rule 5(9) of the SPDI Rules and Section 8(10) of the DPDP Act, we have designated a Grievance Officer to address any discrepancies or grievances regarding the processing of your personal information:

Grievance Redressal Timeline: All grievances received will be redressed expeditiously and within a maximum timeline of 30 days (one month) from the date of receipt.
