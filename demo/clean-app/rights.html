Data Principal Rights Portal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Sections 11, 12, and 14) and DPDP Rules (Rule 14), you have specific statutory rights concerning personal data you have provided or consented to processing.

Required Authentication Particulars

In accordance with Rule 14(1), exercising statutory rights requires identity verification. You must submit your request from your registered account email, accompanied by account credentials or an email verification code (OTP), before records can be disclosed, corrected, or erased.

1. Right to Access Personal Data (Section 11)

You may request a summary of the personal data processed, identities of all third parties or data fiduciaries with whom it has been shared, and details of all processing activities.

2. Right to Correction and Updation (Section 12)

You may request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, completion of incomplete data, or updating outdated information.

3. Right to Erasure (Section 12)

You may request erasure of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or upon withdrawal of consent, unless retention is mandated by law.

4. Right to Nomination (Section 14)

You may nominate an individual who shall, in the event of your death or incapacity, exercise your data principal rights on your behalf.
