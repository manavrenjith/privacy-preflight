## How to read the status column

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OPENED (official)** | I opened the document on an official Indian government domain and read the text. |
| **OPENED (mirror)** | I read the full gazette text, but on a non-government host. The official URL exists but the fetch tool would not open it. Treat as strong but not final until checked against the official PDF. |
| **EXTRACT ONLY** | I saw only a search-result extract of an official page, not the whole document. |
| **NOT OPENED** | I could not open it. Anything I say about it comes from secondary sources and is marked NOT VERIFIED. |

## D. Known errors in secondary sources (do not copy)

1. **easydp.in** says the commencement notification brought *all* sections of the Act into force on 13 Nov 2025. P2 and P1 show a three-stage split. Wrong.
2. **veritect.ai** describes a "startup exemption framework" in the Fourth Schedule of the Rules. The Fourth Schedule (P3) deals with children's data exemptions. Wrong.
3. **S.S. Rana (X3)** gives "13 May 2026" as the 18-month date. Arithmetic from 13 Nov 2025 gives 13 May 2027.
4. **PIB backgrounder (P4)** says data fiduciaries must answer access, correction and erasure requests within 90 days. The Rules text (P3) contains a 90-day cap only for grievance redressal in Rule 14(3). See open-questions.md, Q7.
5. Several explainers (and one Wikipedia-style summary) say the Rules were notified 14 Nov 2025; the gazette dateline is 13 Nov 2025. See timeline.md.
