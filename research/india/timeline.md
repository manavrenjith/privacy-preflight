# India: Commencement Timeline

Retrieved 2026-10-29. Source IDs refer to `sources.md`. Research only; not legal advice.

## 1. Correction to the brief: the staged dates

The brief lists staged dates of **14 Nov 2025, 14 Nov 2026 and 14 May 2027**. The primary texts support **13 Nov 2025, 13 Nov 2026 and 13 May 2027**:

- G.S.R. 843(E) (P2) and G.S.R. 846(E) (P3) both carry the dateline "New Delhi, the 13th November, 2025".
- The India Code copy of the Act (P1, footnote to s.1) records the commencement dates as 13 Nov 2025, "one year from 13th November, 2025" and "eighteen months from 13th November, 2025".
- The 14 Nov date appears in the PIB backgrounder (P4) and in several law-firm articles (X1). The e-Gazette IDs of G.S.R. 843(E) and 846(E) are CG-DL-E-14112025-267647 and -267650, and the Government Press signatures are dated 14 Nov 2025 (10:37:23 and 10:43:53 IST). The issue headers and datelines say 13 Nov. The same one-day lag appears on G.S.R. 892(E) and S.O. 5458(E); that this reflects an upload lag is an inference. (official metadata; second cross-check, 2026-10-29)

Both notifications count the later periods "from the date of publication" of the Gazette. If legal "publication" is taken to be 14 Nov, every later date moves one day. **Recommendation for the skill: use 13 Nov 2026 and 13 May 2027 as the dates, and show 14 Nov / 14 May as a possible one-day variance.** See open-questions.md Q1.

## 3. What commences when: Act (G.S.R. 843(E))

| Stage | Date | Act provisions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 13 Nov 2025 (in force now) | s.1(2); s.2 (definitions); ss.18-26 (Board); s.35; ss.38-43; s.44(1) and s.44(3) |
| 2 | 13 Nov 2026 (one year) | s.6(9) (Consent Manager registration); s.27(1)(d) (Board inquiry into breach of Consent Manager registration conditions) |
| 3 | 13 May 2027 (eighteen months) | ss.3-5; s.6(1)-(8) and (10); ss.7-17; s.27 except (1)(d); ss.28-34; ss.36-37; **s.44(2)** |

Consequences worth stating plainly:

- **All developer-facing duties** (notice, consent, children, security, breach, erasure, rights, transfer) are in Stage 3, so they apply from **13 May 2027**.
- **s.44(2)** is also Stage 3. It omits s.43A of the IT Act and s.87(2)(ob). So the IT Act compensation provision and the rule-making power behind the SPDI Rules fall away on 13 May 2027, not before.
- **s.38** (DPDP prevails over other laws in a conflict) is already in force, but it has little practical bite until the substantive sections commence.
- **s.43(2)**: removal-of-difficulties orders cannot be made after three years from commencement. This is why further wording orders are possible.
- **s.17(5)**: the Government may, before five years from commencement, notify that any provision does not apply to named fiduciaries or classes. None found.

## 4. What commences when: Rules (G.S.R. 846(E), Rule 1)

| Stage | Date | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 13 Nov 2025 (in force now) | Rules 1, 2, 17-21 (title, definitions, Board appointment, terms, meetings, digital office, staff); Fifth and Sixth Schedules by cross-reference to rules 18 and 21 (inferred from cross-references) |
| 2 | 13 Nov 2026 | **Rule 4** (Consent Manager registration and obligations) and, by cross-reference (inferred from cross-references), the First Schedule (r.1 names no Schedule) |
| 3 | 13 May 2027 | Rules 3, 5-16, 22, 23 and, by cross-reference (inferred from cross-references), the Second, Third, Fourth and Seventh Schedules |

Rule 1(3)/(4) say "one year / eighteen months after the date of publication of this Gazette". The corrigendum (verified, file 04) changes that phrase to "in the Official Gazette" in r.1(3) and r.1(4) without naming a day. The Rules text read is pre-corrigendum, as is the text currently served at the MeitY URL.

## 6. Which regime applies in each phase

| Phase | Dates | Applies to an ordinary web/mobile app |
|---|---|---|
| A | Now to 12 Nov 2026 | **SPDI Rules + IT Act s.43A + CERT-In Directions** (and sector rules). DPDP: only Board machinery. |
| B | 13 Nov 2026 to 12 May 2027 | Same as A. DPDP adds only Consent Manager registration, which matters only to firms wanting to be Consent Managers. |
| C | From 13 May 2027 | **DPDP Act and Rules** for all digital personal data. s.43A omitted. CERT-In Directions and sector rules continue. Whether the SPDI Rules survive without s.87(2)(ob) is unresolved (open-questions.md Q8). |

If MeitY shortens the window by amendment (proposed Jan 2026, not notified as of mid-Oct 2026), Phase C would start earlier, at least for some entities. **The skill should read the effective dates from a config file and not hard-code them.**
